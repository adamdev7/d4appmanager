App Manager

Power your business

App Manager is a multi-store Shopify automation platform. It helps ecommerce teams run email flows through Gmail, reply to customers with AI assistance, track shipments, and review analytics and Meta ads — all from one place.

When you connect Gmail, App Manager uses Google OAuth to send and manage business email on your behalf for its Email Automation and AI Email Assistant features. You can disconnect Google access at any time from your settings. How App Manager accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data is described in our Privacy Policy.
